# Product

## Users

Synthetic-package designers, reviewers, accountants, analysts, and agent operators who need business files that are complete, internally coherent, realistically handled, and difficult for the right reasons.

## Purpose

Syn Studios turns artifact realism from a last-minute decoration pass into a maintained design discipline. It helps teams build fuller source packages with credible internal and external records while preserving answerability, provenance, formulas, and human review boundaries.

## Success measures

1. A new package has plausible source footprints and source-specific variation before generation begins.
2. Internal files contain the routine detail and working residue their producers would actually maintain.
3. External and executed records remain formal, restrained, and authoritative.
4. Every template is fact-free, provenance-reviewed, structurally validated, and visibly usable.
5. Difficulty comes from professional synthesis and reconciliation—not missing evidence, arbitrary clutter, or concealed answers.

## Personality

Practical, forensic, human, and disciplined. The system values believable work over polished sameness and evidence over theatrical mess.
